# Legal Review Required

This is a technical issue list for qualified counsel and the Kayven business owner. It is not legal advice and does not state that the website complies with any law.

## Business and jurisdiction

- Confirm the operating entity's legal name, registration details, address, and required website disclosures.
- Determine relevant jurisdictions based on the entity, users, payment, marketing, and data processing.
- Confirm governing law, dispute forum, complaint/escalation channel, and consumer support details.

## Privacy and health-related data

- Confirm actual data categories, purposes, legal bases/consents, retention periods, deletion/export rights, and data-subject request process across the marketing site and external app.
- Review processor/subprocessor terms for hosting, email delivery, analytics, AI, food data, and payments; establish cross-border transfer requirements.
- Review handling of health-related data, minors, security safeguards, incident response, breach notifications, and any required assessments/registrations.
- Reconcile every statement on `/privacy` with deployed behavior; specifically verify headquarters, health data, usage tracking, partner sharing, promotional communications, and deletion/access processes.
- Decide whether the site should use optional analytics/cookies and, if so, implement valid consent and preference enforcement before claiming such use.

## Terms, health claims, and consumer terms

- Review service description, eligibility, account responsibilities, acceptable use, IP, support, suspension/termination, liability language, and dispute terms.
- Review health/wellness disclaimers alongside actual product capabilities and marketing claims; ensure they do not conflict with product claims.
- Verify all claims for food scanning, AI recommendations/coaching, metric counts, exercise library, personalization, outcomes, and data privacy.

## Subscription, pricing, refunds

- Confirm prices, currency, billing periods, discount basis, trial terms, taxes, automatic renewal, cancellation steps/timing, and payment processor behavior.
- Replace physical-goods return language on `/refund` with approved terms for the actual digital service; confirm refund eligibility and legal minimums, timing, processing, and contact/dispute process.
- Replace the pricing FAQ placeholder with consistent, approved refund/cancellation language.
- Ensure cancellation is clear, accessible, and usable; confirm the external account and payment system actually support the advertised actions.

## Marketing and intellectual property

- Verify testimonials, endorsements, statistics, comparative claims, business descriptions, and third-party brand/logo permissions.
- Confirm rights and required attributions for fonts, icons, images, videos, code, and UI packages; review the dependency inventory in [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](./THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md).

## Required approvals before launch

- Written business-owner approval of product, pricing, billing, and refund details.
- Qualified legal review of privacy notice, terms, refund/cancellation terms, health claims, jurisdiction, and required disclosures.
- Technical verification that the shipped website and external app match the approved policies and that user rights/support paths function.
