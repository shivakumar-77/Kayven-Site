03. THE PROBLEM

Modern health apps are fragmented.

People often use different tools for:

* Food tracking
* Calories
* Macros
* Workouts
* Weight
* Water
* Supplements
* Progress
* Health advice

The result is a fragmented picture of their health.

Users can record large amounts of information without receiving meaningful context about what that information means or what they should do next.

Kayven’s perspective

Tracking is useful.

Understanding the data and turning it into action is more valuable.
